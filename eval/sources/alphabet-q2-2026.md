# Alphabet — Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)

Source: [SEC 8-K exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001652044/000165204426000066/googexhibit991q22026.htm)

## Headline results

Consolidated revenues $119.8 billion, +24% Y/Y (+23% constant currency)
— the 12th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth.

## Segment breakdown

- Google Services: $94.5 billion (+15% Y/Y)
  - Google Search & other: $63.3 billion (+17%)
  - YouTube ads: $11.1 billion (+13%)
  - Subscriptions, platforms, devices: $12.9 billion (+15%)
- Google Cloud: $24.8 billion (+82% Y/Y) — acceleration driven by
  enterprise AI Solutions and enterprise AI Infrastructure
- Other Bets: $382 million

## Profitability

Operating income $40.8 billion (+30% Y/Y); operating margin 34%
(+2 percentage points). Net income available to common stockholders
$112.1 billion (+298% Y/Y); diluted EPS $9.11 (+294% Y/Y) — the
extraordinary net income jump reflects $98.0 billion in other income,
primarily unrealized gains on equity securities.

## CEO commentary

Sundar Pichai: "AI investments are redefining what's possible across
every part of our business." Cited 82% Google Cloud growth, ~90% of
Fortune 100 using Gemini Enterprise, 22 billion Gemini API tokens
processed per minute, 950 million Gemini App monthly active users.

## Capital allocation & balance sheet

Raised $49.6 billion via equity issuance in June 2026. Established a
$40.0 billion At-the-Market program (unutilized as of June 30). Issued
$20.3 billion in senior unsecured notes. Quarterly dividend $0.22/common
share; preferred dividend $12.15/share (~$0.60/depositary share).

## Cash flow

YTD operating cash flow $84.9 billion; capex $80.6 billion; trailing
twelve-month free cash flow $53.3 billion (Q2 alone was negative,
-$5.9 billion, due to elevated AI-infrastructure capex).
