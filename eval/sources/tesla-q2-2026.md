# Tesla — Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)

Source: [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf](https://assets-ir.tesla.com/tesla-contents/IR/TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf)

## Headline results

Total revenue $28.236 billion, +26% Y/Y — first quarter Tesla has
crossed $100 billion in trailing-twelve-month revenue.

## Segment breakdown

- Automotive revenue: $20.516 billion (+23%)
- Services and Other: $4.58 billion (+50%), all-time record gross profit
  of $648 million at 14% gross margin
- Energy: $3.14 billion (+13%)

## Margins

Total GAAP gross margin 16.8% (-41bps Y/Y). Automotive gross margin
16.9%, or 16.3% excluding regulatory credits — down from 19.2% in Q1
2026; roughly flat excluding a $230 million Q1 benefit that didn't
repeat.

## Profitability

Operating income $398 million, down 57% Y/Y; operating margin narrowed
to 1.4%.

## Note

This file covers Q2 2026 financial results specifically. A separate,
earlier release (production/delivery report) covers unit volumes — worth
knowing since Tesla splits these into two different documents, unlike
the other four companies here which report volume and financial figures
together.
