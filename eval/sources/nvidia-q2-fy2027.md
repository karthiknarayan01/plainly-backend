# NVIDIA — Q2 FY2027 (quarter ended July 26, 2026)

Source: [investor.nvidia.com press release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)

## Headline results

Revenue reached $96.2 billion, up 106% year-over-year and 18%
sequentially.

## Revenue & margins

- Total revenue: $96.2 billion (Q1: $81.6B; +106% Y/Y)
- Gross margin: 75.0% (GAAP and non-GAAP)
- Data Center revenue: $89.0 billion (+117% Y/Y, +18% Q/Q)
- Edge Computing revenue: $7.2 billion (+27% Y/Y, +13% Q/Q)

## Profitability

**GAAP:** Operating income $63.7B (+124% Y/Y); net income $59.7B (+126%
Y/Y); diluted EPS $2.46 (+128% Y/Y).

**Non-GAAP:** Operating income $64.0B (+124% Y/Y); net income $54.0B
(+118% Y/Y); diluted EPS $2.22 (+120% Y/Y).

## Operating expenses

GAAP opex $8.4B (+55% Y/Y); non-GAAP opex $8.2B (+54% Y/Y).

## Shareholder returns

$26.0 billion returned via share repurchases and dividends in the
quarter. $99.0 billion remaining under the repurchase authorization.
Quarterly dividend declared at $0.25/share.

## Q3 FY2027 guidance

Revenue $108.0B (±2%); gross margin 74.0% (±50bps, GAAP/non-GAAP); GAAP
opex ~$9.2B; non-GAAP opex ~$9.0B; full-year FY2027 tax rate 16-18%.
Guidance excludes China data center compute revenue.

## CEO commentary

Jensen Huang: "AI has reached its inflection point...compute is revenue.
Demand is accelerating." Cited expanded AI lab ecosystems, open-model
adoption, and physical AI momentum.

## Other highlights

Vera Rubin platform ramping to full production across major cloud
providers; Spectrum-6 switches deploying globally; Vera CPU launched for
AI agents; Groq 3 LPX in full production; multiple partnerships announced
toward $500B+ in AI infrastructure financing; Blackwell leading MLPerf
benchmarks.
