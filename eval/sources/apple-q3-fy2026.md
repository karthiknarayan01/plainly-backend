# Apple — Q3 FY2026 (quarter ended June 27, 2026)

Source: [SEC 8-K, Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000018/a8-kex991q3202606272026.htm)

## Headline results

Revenue of $109.4 billion, up 16% year-over-year — the company's
strongest June quarter ever. Diluted EPS of $2.02, up 29% year-over-year.

## Gross margin

Gross margin was 50.1%, including roughly 2 percentage points of
favorable impact from tariff refunds — the same refunds added $0.11 per
share to diluted EPS. Both effects are one-time, not a change to the
underlying margin trend.

## Segment revenue (Q3 FY2026 vs. Q3 FY2025)

- Americas: $45.8B vs. $41.2B
- Europe: $29.4B vs. $24.0B
- Greater China: $18.8B vs. $15.4B
- Japan: $6.6B vs. $5.8B
- Rest of Asia Pacific: $8.9B vs. $7.7B

## Product category revenue (Q3 FY2026 vs. Q3 FY2025)

- iPhone: $54.3B vs. $44.6B
- Mac: $10.4B vs. $8.0B
- iPad: $6.2B vs. $6.6B
- Wearables, Home and Accessories: $7.9B vs. $7.4B
- Services: $30.7B vs. $27.4B

## CEO commentary

Tim Cook cited "double-digit revenue growth across iPhone, Mac and
Services," the introduction of "all-new Siri AI" at WWDC26, and new
child safety features.

## Other highlights

Operating cash flow set a June-quarter record. The active installed base
reached an all-time high across every product category and geographic
segment. The board declared a quarterly dividend of $0.27/share. No
explicit forward guidance was given in the release.
