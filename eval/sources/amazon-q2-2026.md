# Amazon — Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)

Source: [SEC 8-K, Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000024/amzn-20260630xex991.htm)

## Headline results

Net sales of $200.6 billion, up 20% year-over-year. Operating income of
$27.5 billion, up 43% year-over-year. Net income of $62.6 billion, or
$5.75 per diluted share, up sharply from $18.2 billion ($1.68/share) in
Q2 2025.

## Segment performance

- North America: $116.2B net sales (+16% Y/Y); operating income $9.1B
  (vs. $7.5B prior year)
- International: $42.2B net sales (+15% Y/Y); operating income $1.7B
  (vs. $1.5B prior year)
- AWS: $42.2B net sales (+37% Y/Y); operating income $16.6B (vs. $10.2B
  prior year); $169B annualized revenue run rate

## CEO commentary

Andy Jassy: "AWS is booming, growing 36.7% year-over-year in Q2 — our
fastest growth in 18 quarters." AI and Chips businesses each exceeded
$25 billion run rates.

## Growth drivers

AI infrastructure investment — specifically Trainium chip commitments
from Anthropic and OpenAI — drove AWS expansion. Advertising grew 26%
year-over-year. Same-day or overnight Prime deliveries exceeded 40% of
items in the first half of the year. Amazon Bedrock expanded to hundreds
of thousands of customers; Q2 spending on it exceeded all prior quarters
combined.

## Q3 2026 guidance

Net sales of $197.0–202.0 billion (9–12% Y/Y growth). Operating income
of $22.5–26.5 billion, versus $17.4 billion in Q3 2025.
