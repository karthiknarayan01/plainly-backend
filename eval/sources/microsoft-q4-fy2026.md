# Microsoft — Q4 FY2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)

Source: [news.microsoft.com](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/07/29/microsoft-cloud-and-ai-strength-fuels-fourth-quarter-results-4/)

## Revenue & growth

Revenue was $90.0 billion, up 18% (17% in constant currency), ahead of
analyst expectations.

## Profitability

Operating income $40.6 billion (+18%). Net income $35.8 billion (+31%
GAAP); $35.3 billion (+22% non-GAAP). Diluted EPS $4.81 (+32% GAAP);
$4.74 (+23% non-GAAP).

## Full fiscal year

Annual revenue surpassed $331 billion, up 18%. Microsoft Cloud revenue
exceeded $214 billion, up 27%.

## Cloud & AI

Azure revenue grew 43% in the quarter, driven by demand and efficiency
gains, with continued acceleration expected in H1 FY27.

## Copilot adoption

Microsoft 365 Copilot paid seats surpassed 30 million; net seat adds more
than doubled quarter-over-quarter; user satisfaction scores doubled over
the last three quarters.

## Note on reporting structure

An earlier 2026 8-K disclosed Microsoft's updated segment structure
beginning FY2027: two reportable segments, "Agents and Infra" and
"Devices and Consumer" — worth knowing since segment-level historical
comparisons may shift under the new structure.
